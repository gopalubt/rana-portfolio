Who we are

RanaGopal builds web applications and workflow software for businesses. The work is led by Gopal Singh Rana, a Full Stack Developer based in Noida, India.

Gopal translates business workflows across legal, HR and sales into scalable web applications, integrates third-party APIs for multi-channel communication, and works with stakeholders from requirements through delivery.

Before software, he studied biotechnology: a B.Tech from IIT Roorkee and an M.Tech in Biosciences and Biomedical Engineering from IIT Bombay.
